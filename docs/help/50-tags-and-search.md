# Tags, and finding things

What the codes on a story mean, how to stop seeing the ones you do not want,
and where the search looks.

## Tags

Tags follow the shape of StoriesOnline's codes, so they should read as
familiar: what the story is, who is in it, what happens in it. The point of
them here is the same as there — **telling a reader what they are walking
into** before they walk into it.

They are set on **Edit details** for a story, and the whole vocabulary is
browsable at **Tags**.

The vocabulary is curated rather than free: anyone can type a tag that does
not exist yet and **propose** it, and an admin either approves it or merges
it into the one that already meant that. Free tags turn into six spellings
of the same idea within a month.

## Tags you would rather not see

On your **account** page there is a list of tags to hide. Stories carrying
one drop out of the index for you and nobody else. Nothing is deleted or
hidden from anybody: it is your own filter on your own view.

## Searching

The search box in the top bar looks through **story titles and blurbs,
chapter titles and summaries, the current text of every chapter, the
glossary, and every story's bible**.

Only the current text of a chapter is searched, not its history. A phrase
that appeared in three drafts would otherwise bury one real result under
three copies of itself.

## What is waiting for you

The stories index puts what needs you above what merely exists: chapters
with notes waiting on your answer, replies to you, and things you have not
read yet, before the general list.
