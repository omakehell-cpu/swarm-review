# Questions people ask

Short answers to the things that come up, with a link to the longer one.

## Reading and notes

**I opened a chapter and there are no notes, but I know there are some.**
You are in **Read** mode. Press **Review** (**Revise**, on your own
chapter), under the title. If you want
every chapter to open in Read on purpose, that is a setting on your
[account page](/help/your-account).

**I selected some words and nothing happened.**
Check you are in **Review**: **Read** is the story alone, and offers
nothing on a selection. On a phone, lift your finger and wait a moment: the button appears above
the selection. With a keyboard, press **C** once the words are selected.
On your own chapter you can comment too -- it is how you answer a reader
in place.

**Can I change a note after I have posted it?**
Yes: **Edit** under it, as long as it is yours. **Retract** takes it
away, leaving a marker so the replies under it still make sense.

**The author rewrote the chapter. Where did my note go?**
If the words it was on are still there, it moved to the new version with
them. If the author rewrote those words, it stayed on the version it was
about, and the new version says how many notes are waiting back there.
See [versions](/help/reading-and-reviewing).

**What is the difference between Accept and Apply change?**
**Accept** is the author saying "yes, noted" -- the text does not change.
**Apply change** appears on a suggested rewrite and puts it into the
chapter as a new version, then marks the note accepted.

**Who can see that I read a chapter?**
Everyone who can open the story: the initials under each chapter in the
contents are its readers. That is deliberate -- silence means something
different when you know nobody has looked.

## Writing

**I closed the tab without saving. Is it gone?**
Almost certainly not. The editor keeps a **draft** every few seconds, on
the server, and opens it the next time you edit the chapter -- on any
device. See [drafts](/help/writing-a-chapter).

**Why can people not see my changes?**
Because they are a draft. Only **Publish** makes a new version that
readers see. The editor says, above the text, when you have an
unpublished draft.

**The spellchecker keeps marking my characters' names.**
Click the mark and add the name to the story's dictionary, or put the
character in the [glossary](/help/story-glossary), which adds it for you.

**The coloured marks are distracting while I draft.**
Turn **Writing style checks** off, at the top of the checks panel, and
on again when you want them. Or click any single card to hide just that kind. See
[the writing checks](/help/writing-checks).

**Can someone else edit my chapter?**
Only if they are a coauthor of the story and it is their chapter.
Nobody -- not a coauthor, not an admin -- can rewrite a chapter somebody
else wrote.

**I prefer marking up in Word. Can I?**
Yes. **More** on the chapter page has *Word, with the notes as comments*;
comment in Word, then use *Notes from a Word file* at the foot of the
notes to bring your comments back as notes. See
[reading it in Word](/help/reading-and-reviewing).

**How do I get my whole story out as a file?**
At the foot of the story's page: PDF, EPUB, Word, Markdown or plain text,
laid out as a manuscript or as a book.

## The site

**I forgot my password.**
Ask an admin: they can make you a reset link from the admin page. The
login page says the same.

**My account is locked.**
Three wrong passwords in a row lock it. An admin unlocks it from the
admin page.

**How do I invite somebody?**
Ask an admin. They can make a code for anyone, or one tied to a
username, and copy it as a ready-made message.

**Where do I see what changed on the site?**
Under **Help**, in [What's new](/help/changelog). The front page also
tells you once, the first time you come in after a change.

**Is anything I write sent to an AI?**
No. There is no AI anywhere in this app, and nothing you write leaves the
machine the site runs on. See
[where your writing lives](/help/your-account).
