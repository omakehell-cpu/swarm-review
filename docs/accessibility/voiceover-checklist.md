# Checking the site with VoiceOver

A walk through the four things a writer does on Swarm Review, with
VoiceOver on a Mac, in Safari. `npm run a11y` checks the mechanical half
(contrast, names, landmarks). This list is the other half: whether the
page makes sense when you listen to it. Plan on about forty minutes. Do
it after any change to the chapter page, the editor or the notes.

## Before you start

- **VoiceOver on and off:** Cmd+F5, or hold Cmd and press Touch ID three
  times.
- **VO** below means Control+Option held together.
- **Safari > Settings > Advanced:** tick "Press Tab to highlight each
  item on a webpage". Without it, Tab skips links.
- **Rotor:** VO+U, then Left/Right to choose a list (headings, links,
  landmarks, form controls). Down arrow moves through the list and
  Return goes there.
- **Next heading:** VO+Cmd+H. **Read everything from here:** VO+A.
- **Typing into a text box:** VO+Shift+Down to interact with it, and
  VO+Shift+Up to leave it.
- Sign in as a reader who is not the chapter's author, on a copy of the
  site with a story of at least two chapters and a few notes.

Tick a line only when you *heard* it the way it is written, not when you
saw it.

## 1. Arriving

- [ ] On any page, the first thing Tab reaches is "Skip to content", and
      Return on it takes you past the menu.
- [ ] The rotor's landmarks list has: banner, "Site" navigation, main
      and content information. There are no unnamed "region"s.
- [ ] The headings list starts with one level 1 heading that names the
      page.
- [ ] On the front page, a story is read by its title first, then its
      author, then the details.

## 2. Reading a chapter (the most important path)

- [ ] Open a chapter. Tab first reaches "Skip to the chapter", and
      Return on it lands on the first line of the text. The title comes
      before any menu.
- [ ] VO+A reads the chapter from start to end with nothing in between:
      no "link", no "highlight", no note numbers or buttons.
- [ ] A scene break is said as "Scene break", not skipped or read as a
      symbol.
- [ ] In Account > Reading chapters, tick "open in Read mode" and "no
      links in the prose". Reload the chapter: names of characters are no
      longer links.
- [ ] The two chapter menus are heard as "Chapters, before the text" and
      "Chapters, after the text". The arrows at the edge of the screen
      are silent.
- [ ] The reading settings say their value first, for example
      "Large, type size".
- [ ] The link to the next chapter at the end of the text says which
      chapter it goes to.

## 3. Leaving a note

- [ ] Switch to Review mode. The passages that have notes are now
      marked, and only now.
- [ ] Select a sentence with the keyboard (Shift+arrows in the text,
      VO+Return to start and stop selecting). VoiceOver says how many words
      are selected and "Press C to comment". C opens the note box.
- [ ] The kind of note (comment, question, suggestion, praise) is a set
      of labelled choices. The one chosen is said as "selected".
- [ ] A suggestion's "replace with" box has a label.
- [ ] After posting, you can find your note: it is in the headings
      list with your name on it.

## 4. Going through notes on your own chapter

- [ ] In the headings list, each note is a level 3 heading: kind, who
      wrote it, and the first words of the passage.
- [ ] "Go to the passage" moves to that sentence in the text. From
      there, Tab gets you back to the note.
- [ ] A suggestion is read as "replace … with …", not as two blocks of
      coloured text.
- [ ] Tabbing into Accept, Reject or Reply says which note they belong
      to ("What to do with the note by …"). Pressing one says what
      happened, without reloading the page.

## 5. Writing and editing

- [ ] Every button on the editor toolbar is read by its name (Bold,
      Italic, Heading…), and when you are inside bold text it says
      "Bold, selected".
- [ ] Option+F in the text says the formatting at the caret and which
      scene you are in.
- [ ] Pressing Cmd+B says "Bold on" or "Bold off".
- [ ] "Checks as a list" opens a list you can go through with the
      arrows. Each item says what the problem is and the words it is
      about.
- [ ] Opening the scenes and notes drawer moves you into it. Escape
      closes it and puts you back where you were typing.
- [ ] After "Save draft", the page says the draft is saved and that
      readers still see the published version.
- [ ] Restoring a draft reads the restored text, not an empty box.

## 6. Low vision (no VoiceOver needed)

- [ ] Cmd+Plus up to 400% on a 1280-pixel wide window: no sideways
      scrolling except inside the outline table, and nothing covers the
      thing that has focus.
- [ ] System Settings > Accessibility > Display > Increase contrast: the
      borders and quiet text get darker, in all three looks.
- [ ] Each look, light and dark: the focus ring is always visible.

## Writing it down

For each line that fails, note the page, what you pressed, what you
heard and what you expected. That is enough to fix it.
